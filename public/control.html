Preview

With the Fire TV app 1.7 and later and Allow PC control turned on in its MENU, it shows pictures from the TV (about 1 per second per window, at most 4 a second in all), and moving the mouse, clicking or scrolling on a window here does it on the TV. Otherwise it shows the PC's own copy of each window. Video players are muted; web pages may still play sound. It plays every window on this PC too, so it uses more network.
The TV does not take control from the PC. With the Fire TV app 1.7 and later, press MENU (≡) in the Split Screen app and turn on Allow PC control.
